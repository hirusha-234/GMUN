GOOD TO KNOW

07 — FAQ

A few things
you might wonder.

When and where is GMUN'25? +

GMUN'25 took place on 21–22 June 2025 at the Management Development and Training Institute. The opening ceremony was scheduled for 8:00 AM on 21 June, and the closing ceremony for 5:00 PM on 22 June.

How do I register for GMUN'25? +

Registration windows listed on this page are closed. Please contact the organising team for information about future editions.

What is included in the registration fee? +

Please contact the organising team for a confirmed breakdown of what was included in the GMUN'25 registration fee.

Is accommodation provided? +

Accommodation is not included in the standard registration fee. However, GMUN partnered with several hotels near the venue that offered special rates for GMUN delegates. Please contact the organisers for current or future accommodation information.
